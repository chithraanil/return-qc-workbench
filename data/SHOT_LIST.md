# Shot list: 18 claim cases (36 photos + 6 videos)

Tips: phone camera, daylight, plain background, product fills ~70% of frame.
**Dispatch** = shot as the seller at packing. **Return** = shot as the customer.
Use the **same physical item** for both unless the case says "different item".
Keep the original files from the camera (EXIF matters). Save with these exact names into `data/media/`.
